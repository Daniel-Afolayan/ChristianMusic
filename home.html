Meet the artist

Worship made to bring us closer.

Elevation Worship is the musical ministry of Elevation Church in Charlotte, North Carolina. Their songs grow from a shared faith and a desire to help people encounter God through worship.

Their mission is to create music for the church and for anyone looking for hope: songs that point beyond the stage, toward the love of God and the strength we find in community.
